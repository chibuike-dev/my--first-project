Practical Uses of AI in Robotics and Technology

I used an AI tool to perform five different tasks and explored how AI can be useful in robotics and technology.

1. Programming: AI can help create and explain code for robots and other technology projects.
2. Problem Solving: AI can identify errors in programs and suggest solutions.
3. Data Analysis: AI can analyse data collected by sensors and provide useful results.
4. Computer Vision: AI can help robots recognise objects, people, and their surroundings.
5. Automation: AI can control and automate repetitive tasks, making systems faster and more efficient.
